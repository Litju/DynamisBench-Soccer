## Reference design

The LPT model prescribes the vertical motion polynomial `z(t) = 0.4t + t²` and a cable signal `L(t) = L₀ + cos(θ) z(t)` under a fixed effective projection. The closed-form derivative and rigid-load power relation control the finite-difference result. Sensitivity uses the maximum deviation across angle and clock-scale endpoints. Thus the power operand only describes the stated model of the load. The encoder forward model wraps equally spaced electronic counts modulo the declared modulus and reverses the signed physical orientation. The conditional displacement bound uses the two endpoint half-count errors and the declared scale and projection.

The IMU forward model generates body-specific force by rotating field acceleration minus gravity into the instrument frame, applying a fixed cross-axis shear and axis calibration, and adding a constant gyro offset. The horizontal position reference uses a closed-form constant-acceleration trajectory, with a conditional displacement radius of `0.5 b t²` for a declared constant residual field bias `b`. The no-attitude observation has calibrated norm equal to gravity. It is compatible with both a static tilted sensor and an aligned accelerating sensor. Both have zero true angular rate, so the gravity magnitude alone does not resolve the interpretation. The threshold-signature scenario brackets a contiguous low-specific-force interval with native edge samples. The sum of the two edge intervals bounds the difference between the outer sample span and the threshold-crossing duration under the stated bracket assumption.
